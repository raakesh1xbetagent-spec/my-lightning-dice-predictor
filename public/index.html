📚 How Median-Based AI Works

📊 Step 1: Collect Data
Analyzes last 30 game results
📐 Step 2: Count Frequencies
Count LOW, MEDIUM, HIGH occurrences
🎯 Step 3: Find Median
Sort counts → pick middle value → identify group
✅ Step 4: Check Uniqueness
If median is UNIQUE → PREDICT that group
⏳ Step 5: Handle Ties
If counts duplicate or equal → WAITING mode
🔄 Step 6: Retry System
Wrong prediction? Recalculate median with updated data
💡 Why Median-Based?
📊 Median is resistant to outliers and represents the central tendency.
🎯 Prediction only when median is UNIQUE (no ties) for highest reliability.
⏳ WAITING mode ensures we don't predict during uncertain conditions.
🔄 Dynamic retry: After wrong prediction, recalculates median with new data.
